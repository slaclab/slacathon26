The Event

SLACATHON 2026 - Demo is an AI hackathon platform demonstrator. The challenge asks potential participants to apply modern machine learning tools to solving real world accelerator problems: tuning the parameters of a particle accelerator beamline for better performance.

Teams are asked to develop algorithms for: round-to-flat beam transform, emittance tuning, FEL taper optimization, data visualization.

The platform provides a production environment with asynchronous job validation, per-user submission quotas, automatic duplicate detection, full job history, and a public API to work on actual accelerator problems.

Bayesian Optimization
Teams are encouraged to use BO to intelligently sample the search space.
Realistic Physics
Teams will be provided with high-fidelity numerical models and objective functions derived from actual SLAC systems.
Online Leaderboard
Live leaderboard ranking every submission.

Categories

Beamline guru

Beamline gurus are the ultimate masters of accelerator tuning. They specialize in optimizing the full beamline parameters — from emittance and beam size to round-to-flat beam transforms.

FEL whisperer

FEL whisperers possess a unique talent for Free-Electron Laser optimization. They know how to generate maximum pulse energy by adjusting the undulator tapering.

M.A.R.S. explorer

M.A.R.S. explorers venture deep into the Multi-modal Accelerator Real-time System. They combine agentic Skills, simulation, and data visualization to display the most complex regions of machine parameter space.
